# Design references and rationale

This portfolio is not a visual copy of any one website. It combines patterns commonly used by strong academic/robotics researcher homepages.

## Reference patterns

### Shuran Song — Stanford
- concise affiliation + research mission at the top;
- research/publications are the dominant content;
- direct paper / webpage / code links;
- academic readability is prioritized over decorative UI.

Reference: https://shurans.github.io/

### Chelsea Finn — Stanford
- immediate researcher identity, lab affiliation, short research statement, and high-value academic links;
- minimal navigation and low-friction access to CV / Scholar / lab material.

Reference: https://robotics.stanford.edu/~cbfinn/

### Changhao Wang — Stanford / Berkeley robotics
- visible recent-news feed;
- publication-first structure;
- visual paper/project entries with direct paper, project and code links.

Reference: https://changhaowang.github.io/

### Yuke Zhu — UT Austin
- concise statement of research vision;
- clear intersection of robotics, machine learning and vision;
- strong lab/research identity without turning the homepage into a generic software-developer portfolio.

Reference: https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~yukez/

## What was adapted for Tashmoy's site

- A compact research-centric hero with current institution, advisor and research identity.
- A “Latest” strip to surface current papers without making the page feel like a news blog.
- Large visual featured-research blocks for DART and OVITA.
- Custom lightweight visual abstracts rather than stock imagery.
- A publication list designed for quick scanning by faculty, collaborators and PhD/research recruiters.
- Research themes that explain the intellectual through-line: manipulation → safety → learning-enhanced control → language-guided adaptation.
- A sanitized public web CV without residential address or phone number.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS for GitHub Pages reliability and near-zero maintenance.
