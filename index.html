Research profile

Building robot intelligence that is learned, physically grounded, and safe to execute.

I work at the intersection of robot learning, manipulation, planning, and control. My current research focuses on dual-arm manipulation, safe reactive motion generation, learning-enhanced model predictive control, and interpretable language-guided adaptation of robot behavior.

My broader goal is to develop robot systems that can adapt to uncertain, contact-rich environments while retaining the feasibility guarantees and debugging structure of model-based robotics.

Research

Research directions

01

Dual-arm manipulation

Coordinated bi-manual manipulation under contact, kinematic coupling, and execution constraints, with emphasis on non-prehensile and closed-chain tasks.

02

Safe motion generation

Reactive operational-space motion planning with explicit self-collision, inter-arm collision, obstacle, joint, and runtime constraints.

03

Learning-enhanced control

Combining MPC and operational-space control with online dynamics identification, learned models, and reinforcement learning.

04

Language-guided robotics

Open-vocabulary interfaces that transform natural-language intent into interpretable and auditable robot trajectory adaptations.
